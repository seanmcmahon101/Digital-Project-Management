How this works

Projects move through seven stages:

  1. Intake — state the problem & find a sponsor
  2. Discovery — understand today's process, measure the baseline
  3. Define — business case, scope, requirements, targets
  4. Plan — milestones, tasks, risks, RACI
  5. Build & Test — deliver and prove it against requirements
  6. Implement — training, comms, go-live readiness
  7. Benefits & Close — measure the result, learn, close

Each stage has a recommended gate. You can override any gate — the reason is simply recorded, so future-you knows why.
